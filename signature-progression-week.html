Signature Progression Week

A structured roadmap, not isolated lessons

A special package you can book all together — for surfers who want real progress in a single week.

Made for a full week of progress

For surfers staying in Costa Rica who want to make meaningful progress over several days or weeks. Designed for travelers renting villas, Airbnbs, or spending extended time in Tamarindo.

Instead of booking lessons one by one, you book the whole week together — with a plan that connects each session to the next.

Understand what to practice, why it matters, and how to continue improving long after your trip ends.

Request your week

Available elements

  • Daily surf coaching
  • Guided surf tours
  • Video analysis
  • Technical breakdowns
  • Surf theory seminars
  • Wave reading sessions
  • Mobility & recovery guidance
  • Yoga sessions
  • Massage therapy
  • Photo & video packages
  • Personalized progression plan

How it fits with the rest

The Foundation covers the technique work in Tamarindo, The Exploration takes you to other breaks along the coast, and The Immersion mixes surf with Costa Rican adventure. The Signature Progression Week ties them into a single structured plan.

Ready when you are

Tell us your level, your dates and how many of you there are — we'll build the week around it. Pricing is provided on request.
