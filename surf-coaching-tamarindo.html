Who it's for

The Foundation is the starting point at Lean Forward. It works for two kinds of surfer: someone who has never stood on a board and wants a safe, patient introduction to the ocean, and someone who already catches waves but wants the technique broken down properly instead of guessing.

Because the format runs from a one-on-one private session up to a group of three or more, the coaching stays personal at every price point. What changes between the formats is how much of the session is direct attention, not how much care goes into it.

Where it leads

Once you are catching waves consistently in Tamarindo, The Exploration takes you to spots further along the Guanacaste coast, and The Immersion pairs a day of surfing with a half day of exploration.

Not sure you're ready to move on? Read 5 signs you're ready to move beyond beginner surf lessons.

For a full itinerary experience, the Signature Progression Week is the one to look at — a structured roadmap rather than isolated lessons, with video analysis, wave reading sessions and a written plan you keep after the trip.
