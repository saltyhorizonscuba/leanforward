Why the spot changes

Guanacaste has a lot of coastline and no two beaches work on the same conditions. Rather than committing to a destination in advance, the spot is picked on the day so the group surfs where the swell, wind and tide actually line up.

That is the difference between a surf tour and a surf transfer: the point is not the drive, it is arriving somewhere that is working.

For a closer look at each break, read our local's guide to the best surf spots around Tamarindo.

Before you book

The Exploration assumes you can already catch and ride green waves independently and feel comfortable managing yourself in the water.

You don't need to be an expert. Mateo stays in the water with you throughout the session, offering in-water coaching, guidance and safety support to help you progress. The goal is to explore different breaks around the coast, like Playa Grande or Avellanas, while choosing conditions that match your level and comfort.

If you're still building your foundation, start with The Foundation in Tamarindo. The two can also work beautifully together during the same trip.
