## Dan Martin

We love Mateo at Lean Forward Surf Academy!

I'm an older guy (late 50s), wasn't in great shape, and had very little surf experience before my first trip to Tamarindo. I was a little nervous about keeping up. Thought I'd be the slow one in the group, huffing and puffing while younger folks shredded. But Mateo made the whole experience welcoming, encouraging, and genuinely fun.

Mateo has been our instructor on multiple trips now, and the difference from the first lesson to where we are today is night and day. He's incredibly patient and knows exactly how to break things down for beginners and out-of-shape folks like me. He never made me feel rushed or embarrassed, even when I was eating sand or flopping off the board in the whitewater. His teaching style is clear, positive, and tailored, he adjusted everything to my fitness level and gave practical tips that actually worked.

What really sets Lean Forward (and Mateo specifically) apart is how they treat you. From day one, Mateo treated my wife and I like family. He remembers little details about us, asks about our lives back home, and genuinely seems to care. After a few trips, it honestly feels less like booking surf lessons and more like going to visit family in Costa Rica. He builds a rapport so quickly and naturally that it feels like he's known you your whole life. The warmth and personal connection make the whole vacation that much better.

The lessons themselves are excellent, safe, progressive, and focused on building real skills instead of just pushing you into waves you're not ready for. They also have a professional videographer who films your sessions and provides feedback afterward. Seeing yourself on video (the good, the bad, and the hilarious wipeouts) is incredibly helpful for improvement. It's a fantastic touch that most other schools don't offer at this level.

If you're an older traveler, beginner, or just not in peak physical condition, I can't recommend Lean Forward Surf Academy and Mateo enough. They made surfing accessible and enjoyable for us, and we keep coming back because of the people as much as the waves. Tamarindo is a great spot, and this academy is the perfect place to learn (or keep improving) while feeling completely supported.

Highly recommended—5 stars!

The warmth, genuine care, and passion for surfing created such a special connection that we still keep in touch and can't wait to surf with them again.

If you're a nervous intermediate looking to level up, book with Mateo immediately. You'll not only become a better surfer, you'll gain a friend (and basically family) in the process. Absolute legend! 🌊❤️
